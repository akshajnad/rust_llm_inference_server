The following excerpts are taken from a collection of encyclopedia articles about history, geography, sport, music and natural science. Each excerpt is reproduced exactly as it appears in the source, including its section headings, citations and unusual spacing around punctuation. The articles were written by many contributors over several years, so their style varies: some read as narrative history, others as lists of facts, statistics and dates. Names of people, places and organizations are given in full on first mention. Measurements keep their original units. Where an article continues past the end of an excerpt, the text simply carries on in the same voice and at the same level of detail, covering the next events, places or results in order.
